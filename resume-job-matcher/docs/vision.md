# Product Vision

## Problem
Job seekers spend time finding relevant openings and employers have difficulty
surfacing candidates whose experience fits a role.

## Vision
Build a platform that parses a job seeker's resume, identifies skills, and
surfaces explainable, ranked job matches. Employers can publish openings and
review candidate matches.

## Stakeholders
- Job seekers looking for relevant opportunities.
- Employers managing openings and candidate shortlists.
- Administrators maintaining sources, taxonomy, and platform quality.

## Initial scope
User accounts, resume upload and parsing, job-source normalization, ranked
matching, and employer job/candidate workflows. Exact release boundaries and
success measures remain to be agreed.
