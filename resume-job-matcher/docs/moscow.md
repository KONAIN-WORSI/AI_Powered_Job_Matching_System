# MoSCoW Prioritization

This is an initial backlog classification to validate with stakeholders.

## Must have
- Job seeker and employer accounts with role-aware access.
- Resume upload, extraction, and reviewable skill data.
- Searchable jobs and ranked resume-to-job matches.
- Employer job posting and candidate review.

## Should have
- Match explanations and skill-gap summaries.
- Deduplication and scheduled refresh for external job feeds.
- Application and shortlist tracking.

## Could have
- Additional permitted job-source adapters.
- Candidate and employer notifications.
- Match-quality calibration dashboard.

## Won't have in the first release
- Automated applications on behalf of job seekers.
- Unapproved scraping or redistribution of third-party job data.
